I’m Wes Packer. I act, write, make comedy, and make music — usually with a drum kit nearby. This is a home for the work, the ideas, and the practice behind it all.

What I do

Stories, sounds, characters, and questions — collected across a few different stages.

01 / On stage & screenActing

Become someone else.

Character-led work built on curiosity, close listening, and finding the human detail.

02 / On the pageWriting

Follow the thought.

Stories and words that look for the odd, tender, surprising angle on being alive.

03 / In the roomComedy

Find the funny.

Playful, personal comedy — where the ridiculous and the recognisable meet.

04 / In the pocketMusic

Drums & rhyme.

A drummer and rapper drawn to rhythm, live energy, and the feeling of a good groove.

05 / In everyday lifeSpirituality

Practice being here.

Buddhist thought and spiritual practice as a way to meet life with attention, kindness, and a sense of humour.

A work in progress

Making things.
Paying attention.

Creativity and practice aren’t separate worlds for me. Both ask for presence: to notice what’s happening, stay open, and keep showing up even when the result is uncertain.

This space brings those threads together — the performing, the writing, the music, and the quieter questions underneath them.
